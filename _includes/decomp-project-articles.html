Byte-Accurate Decompilations

Reconstructing a game's source makes its systems readable and gives matching projects a demanding test: compile the recovered code back to the original bytes. This section includes the catalogue's decompilation and matching-decompilation projects, alongside assembly reconstructions explicitly labelled as disassemblies. These are projects at different stages, rather than a list of completed, verified byte-identical builds. A general Decompilation label alone does not establish a matching build.

Recompilations

Recompilation offers a route from the original executable to another platform without first recovering hand-written C or C++. The projects below are labelled as recompilations in the catalogue; many also provide native ports. Each entry retains the recorded limitations and supported targets. The guide to creating an N64 recomp explains ROM mapping and runtime integration, while Testing and Automating an N64 Recomp covers validation.

Ports

A native port adapts a game for another platform, opening up different controls, displays and hardware. This section collects projects labelled as native ports that are not also labelled as recompilations. Their notes describe the particular targets and changes recorded in the catalogue; inclusion does not imply a finished or playable release.
